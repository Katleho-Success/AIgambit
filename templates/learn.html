♟️ Chess Fundamentals

♙

Pawn

Moves forward 1 square (2 on first move). Captures diagonally. Can promote to any piece!

♘

Knight

Moves in an "L" shape. The only piece that can jump over others. Value: ~3 points

♗

Bishop

Moves diagonally any number of squares. Stays on same color. Value: ~3 points

♖

Rook

Moves horizontally or vertically any number of squares. Strong on open files. Value: ~5 points

♕

Queen

Combines rook and bishop moves. Most powerful piece! Value: ~9 points

♔

King

Moves 1 square any direction. Must be protected! Can castle with rooks.

🏁 Opening Principles

1

Control the Center

Place pawns and pieces to control e4, d4, e5, d5. Central control = more space and activity.

2

Develop Your Pieces

Get knights and bishops out early. Don't move the same piece twice. Don't bring the queen out too early.

3

Castle Early

Castle within the first 10 moves. It protects your king and activates your rook.

4

Connect Your Rooks

After castling, try to connect your rooks by developing all minor pieces and the queen.
